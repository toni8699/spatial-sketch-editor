## Parent contract (durable, from umbrella)

The creator can author exact architectural dimensions numerically.

- Must support exact straight-wall sizing with an explicit fixed-endpoint choice.
- Must preserve segment identity, valid opening relationships and room topology.
- Must reject an invalid candidate atomically (one history entry per Apply).
- Numeric values are exact and bypass gesture snapping; radians stay canonical
  in documents.
- Must not introduce a general constraint solver, a second geometry pipeline,
  or Scene mutations that compensate for Layout edits.
- Dimension editing belongs to Layout; Arrange keeps its X/Z/Yaw contract with
  read-only dimensions. Preserve sphere/radius and plane dimension semantics.

## Preserved implementation detail (pre-evidence seed)

### Numeric input semantics

- Explicit meters/degrees; finite-number and positive-dimension checks; inline
  rejection reasons; consistent Apply/Escape behavior. Blank input is invalid,
  not zero.
- Layout objects: position X/Y/Z, supported rotation, kind-appropriate full
  dimensions/radius; preserve current world-center and room ownership semantics.
- Manual numeric values are exact and bypass gesture snapping.
- Publish a clear reason when an operation is unsupported rather than presenting
  a nonfunctional field. No floor-wide height/elevation expansion; existing
  thickness/height fields keep their owning scope and validation.

### Room frame (numeric)

- Rooms: numeric frame origin X/Z and yaw via `transformLayoutRoomUnit` and its
  existing pivot semantics. Compute the delta needed to reach the requested
  frame; do not mistake the frame origin for the transform's centroid pivot
  (verified: pivot is the sampled polygon centroid).

### Straight-wall length (semantic candidate)

- Expose exact length in meters with an explicit `Start | End` fixed-endpoint
  choice.
- A semantic candidate moves only the opposite endpoint along the existing line
  direction, preserves the segment ID, updates the closed room boundary through
  the canonical room-editing path, keeps opening offsets in meters, validates
  adjacent segments/openings/topology, and rejects the whole change if invalid.
- `auto-bezier` arc length is a readout only; no numeric curved-wall length
  mutation and no general wall-angle command in P23.
- H1 port candidates (openPlan3D, MIT): `src/lib/utils/wallEditing.ts`
  (`planWallResize` plan→validate→apply structure, `connectedWallEndpoints`,
  join-tolerance/min-length guards) and its test suite as fixture ideas. Its
  20-sample quadratic length approximation and renderer/store architecture must
  not come over.

### Rectangular rooms (width/depth)

- Width/depth along room-local axes, anchored at the current local minimum
  corner, frame unchanged. Enable only for a verified four-line rectangle,
  including rotated rectangles; no bounding-box resize of arbitrary
  polygons/curves.
- Preserve segment IDs and connectivity. Owned objects and Scene contents do
  not scale or move on room resize. Opening offsets stay in meters; reject a
  resize that invalidates them.

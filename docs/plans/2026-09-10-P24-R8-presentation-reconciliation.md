## Refreshed presentation rules

1. Reuse the [visual system](../Design-specs/Design-specs.md) and [shell ownership](../Design-specs/Design-shell-specs.md): existing tokens, Lucide icons, compact grouped controls and continuous Inspector sections. Blue marks selection; strong RGB identifies axes; warning/error colors convey actual status. No new palette, shell, panel framework or viewport decoration pass.
2. Plan offers the R3/R4 X/Z/yaw subset; 3D offers only the selected entity's supported R5/R7 operations. Unsupported handles disappear or disable with a reason while valid selection remains visible. A reason must be available without hovering a disabled element: use adjacent text or the existing selection status, with an accessible association where appropriate. Tooltips supplement icon labels; they are not the only explanation.
3. Reuse viewport/Inspector status for eligibility and operation outcomes. Routine feedback uses status semantics; actionable failures use existing alert treatment. Identify the failed target/count and recovery action when known. Do not repeat the same warning across every surface or turn informational feedback into modal confirmation.
4. Numeric drafts, commits and Escape retain existing `EditorNumberField` semantics. Labels reflect effective units/frame; primary identity, selection count and applicable pivot/frame are readable without guessing from handle color. R5 owns synchronization and pivot behavior; R6 owns mixed material operations. Bulk transform numerics remain follow-up.
5. Helpers, ghosts, snap marks and selection overlays remain transient, editor-only and absent from serialization/Preview/cold visitor. A final visible candidate must match the owner's committed result. R3 support/Y and R4 cancellation gaps cannot be closed by styling.
6. If R9 includes environment/preset authoring, show authored lighting versus session assist explicitly; show loading/failure while R7 retains last-valid rendering. Surface R7's complete all-authored-lights/environment reset warning before its explicit action. No UI-only environment toggle or new reset scope. If deferred, omit the capability and its presentation together.

## Minimum versus optional polish

**Recommended minimum condition for every capability R9 includes:** truthful labels/units/effective state; coherent selection and supported handles; readable unavailable/error/loading feedback; clear material/reset impact where applicable; existing theme tokens, accessible control names and visible keyboard focus. These prevent a user from misunderstanding what will be authored. They are acceptance requirements on the selected vertical slices, not a separate all-editor polish gate.

**Outside the ship gate:** new thumbnail art or catalogue redesign, extra animations, richer helper shading, pixel-perfect spacing/density passes, new iconography, broad tooltip cleanup of unrelated controls, a generalized notification/diagnostics framework, and Scene/asset integrity dashboard. Operation-specific asset failures still need feedback. P25 event/rule/reference diagnostics remain P25/E5. Bulk transforms, new selection tools and advanced material/light controls remain with their earlier capability dispositions; they are not R8 polish.

## R9 acceptance handoff

For the capabilities R9 selects, attach these checks to their implementation briefs:

- Canonical roomless scene in Plan/Arrange and 3D: same selected IDs/count/primary across tree, Inspector and viewport; eligible versus ineligible/mixed/cluster selections show supported handles and reasons. Include explicit legacy compatibility copy without leaking it into canonical labels.
- Move/rotate/scale and numeric edit: actual frame/pivot/step/bypass text agrees with operation state; uniform-scale limitation is clear. Final preview, commit, Escape and accepted view-switch cancel display the R3/R4 result without a new history entry from presentation.
- Floor/support failure, duplicate/group outcome and blocked click/action: feedback matches returned outcome and does not imply complete success for partial/refused work. Intentionally nonprojected lights need no spurious footprint error.
- Selected materials: base/shared/unique/override/mixed/reset and outside-selection impact are understandable; unsupported and failed resources have a reason. Selected lights: units, Unlimited, half-angle diagnostic/repair and finite/illustrative helper distinctions are truthful. Environment/reset checks apply only if included together with R7's full vertical slice.
- Both existing themes, keyboard focus and normal supported panel widths: selection, muted context, warning and disabled states remain distinguishable; labels/reasons are readable without color alone or hover. Helpers never appear in saved data, unsaved Preview or cold publication. No unrelated visitor/relic restyling.

# P23.3 — Wall-hosted openings that fit

**Parent:** [P23 — Layout Depth umbrella](2026-09-07-P23-layout-depth-minimum-build.md)  
**Status:** `implementation-ready` — blocked by Foundation Gate F0 and P23.9 Wall authoring.  
**Evidence:** P23-H1 + H5 complete; wall-first reconciliation ratified 2026-09-09.

## Outcome

Doors and windows are edited as one physical Opening hosted by one canonical Wall. Direct manipulation, numeric editing and portal semantics all operate on the same authored record.

## Canonical authored model

```text
Opening
└─ wallId
```

Retain the strongest existing semantics:

- `offset` is physical meters from canonical Wall start;
- `width`, `height`, `sillHeight`, `kind`, `profile` remain explicit;
- overflow/overlap/vertical-fit errors reject rather than clamp;
- a completed gesture/Apply produces one Layout history result;
- invalid/cancelled/no-op produces none.

The old model `Opening → Room-owned segment` is legacy compatibility only after F0.

## Numeric helpers

Expose:

- Wall length;
- opening start offset;
- width;
- remaining clearances to both Wall ends;
- **Center on Wall**;
- distance-from-start / distance-from-end helpers that resolve back to canonical `offset`.

Do not migrate to normalized `[0,1]` offsets.

## Direct Plan manipulation

Straight Walls only for the minimum.

### Body drag

Dragging the opening body slides it along its hosting Wall.

Preserve unless directly edited:

- Opening ID;
- Wall host ID;
- width;
- height;
- sill;
- profile;
- semantic portal relation.

### Width handles

Left/right handles resize along canonical Wall direction while keeping the appropriate opposite edge fixed according to the interaction contract.

Transient preview uses the immutable gesture baseline. Pointer-up validates/commits once. Escape/pointer cancel restores baseline with no history.

## Fit validation

Validate the complete hosting-Wall opening set, not only the edited record in isolation.

At minimum:

- finite positive dimensions;
- `0 <= offset`;
- `offset + width <= wallLength`;
- valid sill/height relative to Wall/floor vertical semantics;
- no unsupported opening interval overlap on the same Wall;
- no invalid subdivision/host reference;
- compatible profile/kind fields.

No silent clamp or nearest-valid fallback.

## Shared Walls

A Wall between two Rooms hosts **one** physical Opening and produces one canonical compiler cut.

Do not duplicate the Opening per adjacent Room.

Room-side context needed for Plan/3D/portal behavior is derived from the hosting Wall's accepted topology.

## Physical adjacency versus semantic portal intent

These are separate:

```text
Wall-side topology
→ physical adjacent Room(s)

door.connectsRoomIds?
→ explicit semantic inter-Room portal intent
```

For new-schema `connectsRoomIds`:

1. only a `door` may define it;
2. tuple contains two distinct persistent Room IDs;
3. the two Rooms must be the two physical Rooms adjacent to the hosting **boundary Wall at the opening**;
4. the relation never creates adjacency by itself.

A door may omit the relation.

Exterior doors and doors on `partition` Walls normally do not have a two-Room relation.

## Portal editing UI

If P23.3 exposes a portal relation picker:

- candidate Rooms come from the actual accepted Wall-side topology;
- do not present arbitrary nonadjacent Room IDs as valid new-schema choices;
- relation removal is explicit;
- changing `door → window` clears/rejects the portal relation explicitly before commit;
- changing `window → door` does not invent a relation automatically.

## Topology-edit remapping

P23.8 owns split/merge/disappearance remapping. P23.3 must consume that result and never independently guess from Room IDs.

Important cases:

- Room split → relation side maps to the descendant physically adjacent to the opening side;
- Room merge where both relation IDs become one Room → relation is explicitly cleared while physical door survives;
- ambiguous remap → topology edit rejects.

## Legacy compatibility

Existing legacy `connectsRoomIds` may name Rooms that are not physically adjacent under the new topology contract.

P23.0 compatibility rules apply:

- keep old relation readable in legacy read-only/runtime mode;
- do not silently clear it on load;
- do not make an old publication fail solely for violating the new contract;
- block new-schema Save until the relation is explicitly resolved/removed.

Do not apply the stricter new-schema validator directly to raw legacy data before compatibility decoding.

## Wall subdivision interaction

P23.8's Wall split rules are authoritative:

- opening before split stays on retained Wall;
- opening after split moves to new Wall and rebases offset;
- split through opening interior rejects;
- Opening ID survives deterministic host rebasing.

P23.3 must not maintain a competing split-offset implementation.

## Snapping

Consume P23.2 snap infrastructure.

Opening-specific candidates may include:

- Wall end-clearance targets;
- Wall midpoint / centered placement;
- existing opening edges where useful;
- grid only where appropriate to meter-offset editing.

Acquisition/visual guides are transient; opening fit remains model-space validation.

## Curves

Existing curved legacy openings remain compatibility-supported according to F0/H1/H3 fidelity rules.

New direct body/width manipulation is straight-Wall-only in the minimum. Do not resample curves into an approximate second placement model.

## Tests

- Center on Wall yields exact meter offset;
- distance-from-end converts correctly;
- body drag preserves non-edited fields and commits once;
- width handles preserve host/ID and commit once;
- endpoint overflow rejects rather than clamps;
- overlapping openings reject atomically;
- vertical-fit invalid candidate rejects;
- one shared Wall + one Door produces one authored Opening/compiler cut;
- valid new-schema portal relation requires actual two-sided Wall adjacency;
- nonadjacent new-schema relation rejects;
- exterior/Partition door may exist without relation;
- door→window handles relation explicitly;
- Room split remaps portal to adjacent descendant;
- merge collapsing relation endpoints clears relation explicitly;
- ambiguous remap rejects;
- legacy nonadjacent relation remains compatibility-readable but blocks new Save;
- Wall shrink from P23.1 cannot leave an invalid opening;
- Wall split rebase behavior matches P23.8 exactly;
- Undo/Redo restores exact Opening host/offset/portal state.

## Non-goals

- door leaf animation/open-close state;
- hinge/handedness/swing semantics unless separately authored;
- automatic Camera edges from doors;
- inferred portal relations from proximity;
- normalized offsets;
- curved-Wall direct manipulation;
- duplicate physical openings for adjacent Rooms.

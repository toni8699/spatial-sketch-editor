# P23.6a — Architecture Inspector + canonical hierarchy reconciliation

**Parent:** [P23 — Layout Depth umbrella](2026-09-07-P23-layout-depth-minimum-build.md)  
**Status:** `seed` — high-level scope only. Depends on P23.6; P23.7 depends on this slice.  
**Evidence:** P23-H2 complete; wall-first reconciliation ratified 2026-09-09.

## Why this slice exists

PR #23 completes P23.6 (single-Wall presentation, canonical Wall/Junction
selection, drafting UX, dimensions, diagnostics) while the wall-first left
hierarchy stays transitional: Rooms are represented through the read-only
`wallFirstRooms` bucket, document-global Walls have no normal tree rows,
and canonical Openings/Junctions/LayoutObjects are not first-class
hierarchy rows. The legacy `Room → Architecture → Wall` tree cannot simply
be restored — a physical Wall is document-global and may bound two Rooms.
This migration debt is intentional, and it must land as P23.6a before P23.7
closeout validates the actual final hierarchy/Inspector rather than the
transitional bridge.

## Scope (high-level only)

- Redesign the right Architecture Inspector around the selected canonical
  entity rather than dumping the complete topology.
- Canonical Wall click → canonical Wall Inspector directly.
- Canonical Junction / Opening / Room / LayoutObject selection uses the same
  existing `LayoutSelection` authority.
- Replace the wall-first read-only hierarchy bridge with an
  ownership-truthful hierarchy for document-global Walls/Openings/
  LayoutObjects.
- Do not duplicate a shared physical Wall under multiple Rooms.
- Junction/topology inventory stays subordinate/advanced, not the dominant
  normal UI.
- No second selection store, hit path, topology model, compiler, or history
  path.
- Retain LayoutDocument / SceneDocument ownership boundaries.

P23.6a is a UX/presentation + canonical hierarchy reconciliation slice, not
another geometry migration.

## Non-scope for this stub

No detailed design is invented here. The external design proposal will be
reconciled and this stub promoted to an implementation-ready child plan
separately after P23.6 merges.

# P23B.6 S3 comparator reconciliation

**Decision:** the prior M-3m exclusion is superseded; §11 A-1 did not fire under a D-5-conforming comparator. M-3m is not re-landed here. The owner must authorize any production integration.

The committed S3 A/B probe ran in one production Node process at clean head `f923073101b30c097aaedeca4f9e89505cb36bfd` (Node v26.7.0, Apple M2), with five warmups and fifteen samples. It used four 40-Wall fixtures, the accepted single-Wall move and whole-Room bridge, twenty fresh compiled generations per action, and one prepared reference generation. Full provenance and timing distributions are in the [A/B data](2026-09-26-P23B.6-S3-comparator-reconciliation-data.json). No baseline or ratchet was written.

The S1 `Object.keys` comparator and JSON-string ceiling were faster than `deepEqualOwnData` on the same prepared inputs. The S1 comparator was faster than the JSON-string comparison on every fixture/action. The comparator matrix passed for the existing refusal cases, including changed future fields, and a one-time structural check confirmed compiled Walls and resolved ends are plain JSON-like data across the four fixtures. That guard makes S1's cheaper equality appropriate for these inputs and fails if a future compiled field breaks the shape assumption.

The decision is mechanical: S1's comparator is below 50% of skipped-build cost on every fixture and has positive accepted-move net savings on all four (about 9.8–18.1 ms). The JSON-string ceiling also stays below 50% and has positive accepted-move net savings on all four; the S1 comparator is cheaper than that ceiling in every row. Whole-Room bridge results are reported separately and do not count as accepted-edit admission.

The earlier S3 probe used `deepEqualOwnData`, whose prototype, symbol, descriptor, cycle, and typed-array checks cost materially more than the S1 comparator on these plain data records. Its 89.9–106.0% range established that this strict comparator failed the gate; it did not establish that M-3m failed. The old [S3 abandonment record](2026-09-26-P23B.6-S3-abandonment-record.md) remains as historical evidence and is superseded by this record. The new S1a post-S-R re-gate is recorded in [its admission record](2026-09-26-P23B.6-S1a-post-SR-regate.md).

Value framing is advisory only: `mesh-prebuild` is roughly 9–20 ms against 34–2,235 ms releases. The gate remains mechanical. **Recommendation to the owner:** authorize S3 in this PR, since it was already conditional scope in the ratified plan and the corrected comparator passes; no production integration is included until that authorization is given.

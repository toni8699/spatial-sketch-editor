### Five-layer routing

The JSON record carries the complete per-class layer-to-mark mapping and every routed mark's p50/p95. The repeated routing is:

- **Authored documents:** `preview-install`, `gesture-commit`, `restore-project-clone`, `restore-reactive-write`, `baseline-restore` (when reached by that action class).
- **Compiled geometry:** `preview-compile-reused`.
- **Prepared meshes:** `mesh-prebuild`, `restore-mesh-install`.
- **Adapter resources:** no marks; this was a Plan-only capture.
- **Reactive presentation:** `plan-render-model`, `plan-salience`, `svg-attributes`, `plan-svg-context-ink`, `plan-presentation`.
- **Unattributed remainder:** retained per class in the JSON record, including selection/snap/proposal work, preflight/topology, acceptance compile, and commit/restore terms that are not assigned to the five admitted layers. Marks outside action windows are retained as explicit remainder counts; no nested distribution is summed.

Straight-to-curved p50 deltas: in Node DEV, warm proxy Plan model rises from 4.16 ms on straight to 19.47 ms on all-curved (raw rises from 0.096 to 0.314 ms); in Node PROD, warm proxy rises from 2.46 to 16.91 ms (raw 0.123 to 0.871 ms). In the browser rigid-drag class, `plan-render-model` p50 rises from 2.6 ms on straight to 6.8 ms on all-curved, while `mesh-prebuild` rises from 17.7 to 27.8 ms. Percentiles have visible scheduling outliers on curved captures; they are advisory and remain separate by measure.

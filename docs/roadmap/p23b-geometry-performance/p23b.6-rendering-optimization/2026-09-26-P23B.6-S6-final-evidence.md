## Mark coverage of each release boundary

For each measured action on its expected path, the coverage calculation located the `release` boundary and gathered contained marks. For each mark with recorded numeric exclusive-self, it subtracts the mark's recorded child intervals; the resulting intervals are unioned per action, so nested or overlapping marks are never added twice. A mark occupying at least 95% of that action's release interval is classified as boundary-sized and excluded from layer terms; eligible descendants remain in the calculation. This 95% classification only identifies boundary-sized labels, it is not a target. Coverage and uncovered milliseconds are calculated per action and then summarized by nearest-rank p50/p95.

- A canonical generation is strongly held only by live state and document-history entries. Prepared meshes and future derived artifacts (including pick/snap indexes and contextual fragments) are weakly keyed by their generation and do not keep it alive.
- Shared Wall meshes are immutable and remain reachable only through generations whose prepared sets use them; H-6 proves collection with the last generation. The comparator uses the whole compiled Wall, floor elevation, resolved ends and builder-signature tag. A changed added field refuses; compiler output must remain plain JSON-like data.
- GPU resources stay under renderer lifecycle ownership and never enter history or snapshots. This slice makes no GPU or presented-frame claim.
- Future view/session trails identify a generation by canonical ID plus generation number, never by object identity, and never enter document history. Such a P26 trail is not implemented or claimed here.
- No camera, projection, selection, hover, or other view state enters a canonical compile or prepared-mesh key. P23B.4 key grammar, P23B.5 reuse store and ratchet, P23B.7 verdict scope and S6/S7 contracts, single chronological history stack and `HISTORY_LIMIT = 100` remain unchanged.
